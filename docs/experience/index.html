Research Experience

Undergraduate Research Assistant, AI in Education — University of Montana

Jan 2026 – Present · Mentor: Dr. Zedong Peng, Department of Computer Science

Studied whether LLM tutors give responses that suit a learner's knowledge and place in a course, not just correct ones. Developed the Pedagogical Suitability Index (PSI), a composite metric for scoring the pedagogical fit of tutoring responses, and used it to evaluate and improve the output of four LLMs.

  • Designed the PSI evaluation metrics: temporal violation, knowledge distance, cognitive load, and Bloom's taxonomy alignment
  • Applied PSI across 240 evaluations of four LLMs; PSI-guided regeneration, built by collaborators, improved 51 of 62 low-scoring responses (82.3%)
  • First author; presented the paper at IEEE IRI 2026 in Seattle, WA

Housing Affordability in Recreation-Amenity Counties (independent) — University of Montana

Aug – Sep 2026 · Mentor: Dr. Jesse Johnson, Geospatial Data Science course

Tested whether counties with strong outdoor-recreation access and little high-wage industry are unaffordable for local workers, across 2,753 U.S. counties.

  • Built a home-price-to-local-wage metric (Zillow, BLS QCEW) and a recreation amenity index from federal-land polygon overlays and recreation employment (GeoPandas, statsmodels, QGIS)
  • Found a significant amenity × industry interaction (p < 10-13): amenity's effect on unaffordability was ~3× larger where high-wage industry was scarce, acting through prices rather than wages
  • Caught and corrected a boundary-data error that overstated eastern national-forest land by up to 55 percentage points
