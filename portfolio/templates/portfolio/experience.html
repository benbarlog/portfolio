Undergraduate Research Assistant, AI in Education — University of Montana

Jan 2026 – Present · Mentor: Dr. Zedong Peng, Department of Computer Science

Studied whether LLM tutors give responses that suit a learner's knowledge and place in a course, not just correct ones. Developed the Pedagogical Suitability Index (PSI), a composite metric for scoring the pedagogical fit of tutoring responses, and used it to evaluate and improve the output of four LLMs.

  • Designed the PSI evaluation metrics: temporal violation, knowledge distance, cognitive load, and Bloom's taxonomy alignment
  • Applied PSI across 240 evaluations of four LLMs; PSI-guided regeneration, built by collaborators, improved 51 of 62 low-scoring responses (82.3%)
  • First author; presented the paper at IEEE IRI 2026 in Seattle, WA
