GNSS Interference Hotspots

Question: Where is GNSS (GPS) interference spatially clustered, how persistent are those clusters over time, and do the detected hotspots survive rigorous statistical treatment, or are they artifacts of aircraft traffic density and receiver coverage?

Aircraft broadcast ADS-B messages that include navigation-integrity indicators (NIC/NACp). When GPS is jammed or spoofed, the share of aircraft in an area reporting degraded integrity rises. This project uses daily H3 hexagon counts from GPSJAM (built on ADS-B Exchange data) to study interference in the Baltic / Kaliningrad region and a contrast region.

  • Spatial weights built from H3 hexagon topology, with handling for islands and sparse coverage
  • Global Moran's I time series and Local Indicators of Spatial Association (LISA) cluster maps, with false discovery rate correction
  • Empirical Bayes rate smoothing and minimum-aircraft thresholds to avoid fake hotspots from small samples
  • Hotspot persistence maps and resolution-sensitivity analysis (modifiable areal unit problem)
  • Stretch goal: an independent NIC-based interference pipeline built from raw adsb.lol ADS-B traces, validated against GPSJAM

Terrain Masking & Least-Exposure Routing

Using real USGS 3DEP elevation data around Missoula, MT, this project models where notional ground-based sensors can see low-altitude drones, then computes routes that minimize the chance of detection. It is a simplified line-of-sight simulation on public data, not a realistic threat assessment; all sensor sites and parameters are notional.

Hypothesis: least-exposure routes pass through lower, more rugged terrain (valleys, draws, lee sides of ridges) than shortest-distance routes between the same endpoints, and the effect weakens as flight altitude increases.

  • Per-sensor minimum-visible-altitude rasters computed with a radial horizon sweep
  • Probabilistic detection model and a survival-based path cost (−ln(1 − P)) solved exactly with Dijkstra / A*
  • Paired Wilcoxon signed-rank tests across randomized sensor layouts, start–goal pairs, and three flight altitudes
  • Interactive Observable notebook with an altitude slider, exposure maps, and path overlays
  • Planned extensions: greedy sensor-placement optimization, cost-to-go flow fields, and 3D altitude layers

Housing Affordability in Recreation-Amenity Counties

Tested whether counties with strong outdoor-recreation access and little high-wage industry are unaffordable for local workers, across 2,753 U.S. counties.

  • Built a home-price-to-local-wage metric (Zillow, BLS QCEW) and a recreation amenity index from federal-land polygon overlays and recreation employment (GeoPandas, statsmodels, QGIS)
  • Found a significant amenity × industry interaction (p < 10-13): amenity's effect on unaffordability was ~3× larger where high-wage industry was scarce, acting through prices rather than wages
  • Caught and corrected a boundary-data error that overstated eastern national-forest land by up to 55 percentage points
